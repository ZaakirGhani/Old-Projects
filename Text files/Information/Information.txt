A learning curve is a mathematical concept that graphically depicts how a
process is improved over time due to learning and increased proficiency.
The learning curve theory is that tasks will require less time and resources
the more they are performed because of proficiencies gained as the process
is learned. The learning curve was first described by psychologist Hermann
Ebbinghaus in 1885 and is used as a way to measure production efficiency and to forecast costs.
